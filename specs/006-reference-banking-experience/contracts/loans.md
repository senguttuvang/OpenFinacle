# Core-Backed Personal Loans

Define the first lending capability: customer application and declared affordability, officer assessment, independent core registration/approval/disbursement, and owned loan holdings/schedules. Execution: `of-9hc.15`; foundation: `.15.1`. This is a staged implementation contract, not a delivered loan journey or observed bank form.

## Evidence And Backend Choice

Evidence date: 17 October 2026. Official [ICICI application information](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/loans/personal-loan/application-process) supports application/document/assessment discovery only. No authenticated loan form, credit enquiry, proprietary eligibility policy or staff page was inspected. Fields, demo prices and role decisions below are inferred OpenFinacle rules, not ICICI terms.

The maintained [Fineract 1.15 documentation](https://fineract.apache.org/docs/1.15.0/) and [API catalogue](https://fineract.apache.org/docs/legacy/) establish the existing core's lending capability. Pinned [LoansApiResource](https://github.com/apache/fineract/blob/1.15.0/fineract-provider/src/main/java/org/apache/fineract/portfolio/loanaccount/api/LoansApiResource.java) confirms schedule calculation, external-ID enquiry and separate application/approval/disbursement commands. Pinned [product](https://github.com/apache/fineract/blob/1.15.0/integration-tests/src/test/java/org/apache/fineract/integrationtests/common/loans/LoanProductTestBuilder.java) and [application](https://github.com/apache/fineract/blob/1.15.0/integration-tests/src/test/java/org/apache/fineract/integrationtests/common/loans/LoanApplicationTestBuilder.java) integration builders identify product/accounting and linked-savings inputs. This is source evidence, not successful adapter or live accounting verification.

Reuse Fineract for products, amortization, portfolio state, disbursement and repayment postings. Do not implement an alternative interest engine or D1 loan ledger. D1 owns application terms, immutable quote snapshots, assessment/authorization/audit events, operation reservations and confirmed read models. Generic request fulfilment never creates a loan. All money/customers remain independently invented demonstration data. No credit bureau, real lender, external payment, PAN, Aadhaar or real financial document is required.

## Product, Quotes And Affordability

The proposed unsecured INR demo product permits ₹10,000–₹5,00,000 in ₹1,000 steps and 12/24/36 monthly installments. Use a fixed illustrative nominal 12% annual rate, declining balance and equal installments, with no fees, penalties, down payment, tranche, automatic repayment or recalculation. The actual core quote determines dates and amounts; no local EMI estimate is accepted as its substitute. Product/accounting configuration must be explicitly checked against the running pinned engine before activation.

Customer inputs: principal, tenure, purpose (home/education/medical/other), whole-rupee monthly income, existing monthly commitments and living expenses. Income is ₹5,000–₹10,00,000; commitments/expenses are nonnegative, bounded and must leave a surplus. Unknown fields fail. These are declared examples, not independently verified income or a credit score.

An owned quote binds the exact terms, bank/client/savings/product IDs, expected Indian disbursement date, injected quote time and a fifteen-minute expiry. It expires at that deadline or Indian midnight, whichever comes first. A changed term/account/product requires a new quote. Returning a schedule does not submit an application, reserve funds, approve credit or disburse.

The normalized schedule must have exactly the chosen number of sequential, strictly date-ordered installments after expected disbursement, INR minor units, exact principal/interest/repayment sums and per-row component equality. Reject incomplete schedules, fractional minor units, invalid dates, mismatched principal, unsupported currency or charges. The core adapter must explicitly verify zero fee/penalty components before normalization, not silently drop them. Maximum installment, rather than average EMI, is compared with income less commitments and living expenses. Passing this declared-surplus check never means automatic approval; officer assessment and independent authorization remain necessary.

## Workflow And Core Authority

Customer submits a still-valid immutable quote. Officer may recommend or reject; customer may cancel submitted/recommended applications before core registration and while no operation is unresolved. Approver may reject a recommendation or independently register it in Fineract. Registration, approval and disbursement are separate explicit reviewed instructions; a proposed transition is not success until its required core result is verified. Preserve distinct submitted/recommended/registered/approved/active/rejected/cancelled states and their immutable histories.

Only the officer recommends; only the separate approver registers, approves and disburses. Server-side bank/client/account/product ownership, current version, original role/key/payload, reason, quote identity and permissible state are mandatory. Invalid maker/checker/core links close actions. An executing or uncertain core operation locks every further mutation, including cancellation/rejection. Terminal applications cannot resume through this contract.

Expired pre-registration quotes cannot be silently refreshed. Customer can cancel and explicitly submit a fresh quote/application; no new core loan has yet been created. Before any later-date core stage is exposed, define renewed customer review/consent, core date compatibility and safe withdrawal/return handling. Do not silently replace accepted schedules or force stale dates through the engine.

The intended disbursement is a real core transfer into the same owned synthetic savings account, not a D1 allocation or an external settlement. Verify the linked account, currency, loan/client/product identity, full amount and core transaction/transfer correlation. Refresh the existing financial saved view after confirmed posting. If that refresh fails, report the confirmed posting and stale synchronization separately; never relabel a posted loan failed or post again. Subsequent savings and loan balances always come from Fineract.

## Persistence, Recovery And Release Gates

Reserve a durable operation and stable core command identity before each write. One active operation per application; guard competing cancellation/registration and decisions atomically. Keep original command input and actor across reload. Pinned [application properties](https://github.com/apache/fineract/blob/1.15.0/fineract-provider/src/main/resources/application.properties) use `Idempotency-Key`, matching the existing adapter; this source takes precedence over a misspelled header in public prose. Idempotency is defense in depth, not permission to replay ambiguous financial writes.

After a lost/unreadable response or persistence acknowledgement, inspect the core read-only by stable external identity and corroborating state/transaction data. Never automatically repeat the POST. A missing external-ID read does not prove an earlier write cannot still complete. Preserve unresolved status and block new instructions until the original outcome is established. A current active status alone is insufficient proof that our requested savings disbursement occurred. No write-to-mock fallback or automatic reset exists.

Before implementing/exposing disbursement, prove the pinned command's supported correlation fields, matching transfer/loan transactions and outcome reconciliation through adapter tests and an isolated real-core scenario. Product creation uses the sanctioned CLI/admin application boundary, verified existing GL mappings and idempotent configuration; never raw provider storage access. No cloud resource/plan expansion. Application rollback preserves additive workflow schemas and core records; no automatic deletion, reversal, restore or replay.

Use the existing Effect/Schema/Store/Core boundaries and architecture fitness gates. Keep vendor DTOs behind an adapter translation boundary; frontend consumes normalized owned contracts. Add no competing app framework, ORM, loan-math dependency or design system. Existing approved typography, forms and customer/branch identities remain authoritative.

Deliver and verify domain/quote rules, product/core adapter, atomic workflow/read models, customer/staff forms and recovery, then the full real-core journey in coherent reviewed units. Parent completion requires exact-source local/static/container/browser and private PR/main CI, protected migration, live synthetic authorization/disbursement, posting/balance invariants, persistent holdings/history, reload/journey retention and honest source comparison. Repayment/statements/servicing require linked follow-up contracts and verification; they are not delivered by the origination foundation.
