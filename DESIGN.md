# OpenFinacle design

The public landing and `/discover` use Persuade mode: a product magazine, not an assessment dashboard. Preserve local Manrope/IBM Plex Sans and the navy/cobalt identity. A warm paper cover, staggered actual product screens, asymmetric feature spreads, household-planning imagery, a dark production-path band and a purple agent-connected diagram create an uneven editorial rhythm. Use short paragraphs and real learn-more destinations (`/capabilities`, `/roadmap`, `/build-story`). One compact comparison section has eight selected areas. Detailed evidence remains on `/assessment` behind an understated footer link; release/test/verification framing is absent from the landing.

The build recipe is the main interactive feature: People, Ambition, AI & approach, Impact & ROI. Team presets and sliders make exploration approachable; output remains explicitly conditional. Intern/developer/expert monthly INR pay comes from the user's assumptions. USD standard short-context API rates are dated and sourced; Sol6.1/Opus5.5 each offer low/medium reasoning scenarios. No automatic currency conversion or invented ROI: benefits and an explicit user exchange rate are required. Team capacity factors, scope ranges, AI task allocation, drafting savings and review/coordination allowances are modelling assumptions, documented on the build-story page. API price and token volume never multiply capability. Broad core replacement is not estimated.

Inner narrative pages inherit the paper system in Read mode. The retained `/assessment` keeps its existing report system. Shared Radix FormSelect, large type, keyboard-scrollable native tables, reduced motion and 200% text/zoom reflow apply throughout. Existing banking interactions and all three late demo actions remain intact.

Operate mode: customers and branch staff must read financial values, understand state, and complete familiar banking tasks. The user requested a modern, colorful professional experience alongside Xero and Payoneer, with comfortable large text. The separate extra-large toggle was removed at the user's request on18October2026.

The shared system uses a light workspace, white navigation, cobalt actions and account summaries, with restrained mint, amber and lilac sections. Values and status remain legible without relying on hue. Account enquiry separates available funds, customer identity and account facts. Branch overview colors identify different kinds of work; counts always come from existing records.

## Tokens and use

- Body: IBM Plex Sans, 18px at the browser's normal 16px setting. Enlarged-text and browser-zoom reflow remain verified. Headings and prominent values: locally served Manrope.
- Type uses rem units; headings approximately 36px, section headings 22px, main controls 17–18px, supporting information 15–17px. Minor system metadata may be smaller.
- Ink `#172b4d`, secondary `#52627a`, action `#2458d3`, account surface `#173d86`, workspace `#f2f5fc`, rule `#dae2ef`.
- Account surface secondary text `#d4e3ff`; normal colored status labels retain explicit words/icons.
- Major panels: 16px corners, 24–28px spacing, simple borders. Primary controls at least 50px high; horizontal navigation scrolls within its own container on narrow screens.
- No separate text-size control or device preference is applied. Browser text resizing and zoom remain available.
- The demo persona picker uses Radix Select, matching the existing Radix dialogs. Its portal avoids sidebar clipping, options have 50px targets and a selected checkmark, and the busy state prevents role changes. Keep keyboard navigation, typeahead, Escape and focus return.
- Responsive account facts stack, dashboard columns collapse, forms use one column, and action controls wrap. Preserve local fonts, reduced-motion support and visible keyboard focus.

## Public visual research

Reviewed October 17, 2026. These are public marketing/product images, not authenticated access or full product audits. Their assets are references only and are not shipped.

- [Xero dashboard](https://www.xero.com/ae/accounting-software/dashboard/) and [new homepage](https://www.xero.com/ca/campaign/new-homepage-navigation/): prominent financial summaries and direct task access. Retain those useful hierarchies without inventing charts.
- [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/): published desktop dashboard image shows grouped balances, clear transaction rows and distinct work areas.
- [Wise team finances](https://wise.com/ca/business/manage-team): clear permissions, approval and spending controls; its public imagery separates identity from actionable settings. A public [demo entry](https://wise.com/business-demo) is available; no claim of a full demo walkthrough.

The product remains an explicit synthetic banking demonstration. This presentation change does not claim reference parity or introduce financial/product capabilities.
